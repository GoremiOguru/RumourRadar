1. Executive Summary

The standard approach to fintech integration in civic tech—paying citizens token micro-bounties for submitting evidence—creates fatal misaligned incentives. It encourages Sybil attacks, manufactured evidence, and introduces complex KYC obligations for anonymous citizens.

RumourRadar inverts this paradigm. Rather than paying for evidence, we use BMONI to monetize access, institutional threat intelligence, and verified newsroom support. This preserves 100% evidence integrity while embedding BMONI as an indispensable B2B revenue and micro-donation infrastructure.

2. Architectural Paradigm Shift

Dimension ❌ Evidence Bounty Model ✅ RumourRadar + BMONI Model
Evidence Integrity High Risk: Users game prompts to farm rewards. Pristine: 100% deterministic & LLM-grounded.
BMONI Usage Forced: Micro-payouts to unverified users. Native: NGN Virtual Accounts & Verified Bank Rails.
Business Model Unsustainable: High cash burn on payouts. High MRR: B2B SaaS Subscriptions for Brands & Media.
Compliance / KYC Complex: Requires citizen BVN registration. Streamlined: Institutional onboarding via BMONI.

4. Technical API Integration Blueprint

A. Virtual Account Provisioning for Institutional Subscriptions

When an enterprise or newsroom subscribes, RumourRadar initiates the BMONI onboarding and assigns a persistent NGN Virtual Bank Account:

POST /v1/users/{userId}/smart-wallets/{smartWalletId}/onramp/vba/nigeria Response: { "accountNumber": "9928371822", "bankName": "Wema Bank / Providus", "accountHolder": "RumourRadar - Access Bank PR Desk", "status": "active" }

Micro-donations routed to accredited news organizations resolve through BMONI’s three-stage Nigerian bank settlement flow:
